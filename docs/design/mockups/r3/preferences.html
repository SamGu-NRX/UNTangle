UNTangle Week preferences Round 3. One direction: the painted grid for hard limits and picture cards for leanings, in a popover from Week's top bar (a bottom sheet on phone). Desktop and phone share one state.

Everything works: paint the grid, drag the sun and moon, open a card and drag its slider (the classes behind move), step the credit limit, lock or skip a section, open More settings, close with the X, Escape or a click outside, and reopen from Preferences. On the phone, drag the grabber down to dismiss.
