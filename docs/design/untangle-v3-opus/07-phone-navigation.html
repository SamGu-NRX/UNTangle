Three tabs, three spans of time

Courses, Week and Day show the same plan at different zoom. Switching tabs is instant and keeps each tab's scroll position and open sheet.

Where you land Hypothesis

During term, if a class is still ahead today, the app opens on Day with the leave-by time. Otherwise it reopens the last tab you used.

One switcher

The schedule pill in the header opens a sheet of named schedules for that term. Picking one never leaves the tab you're on. No tab carries its own schedule tabs.

Sheets, not pages

Course detail, sections, preferences and export open as sheets: half height first, full on drag. Detail sheets dim the screen; the Day sheet does not, so the map stays live.

One sheet deep

A second sheet replaces the first one's content instead of stacking. Swipe down, tap the dimmed area, press Esc or the system back gesture to close it.

Back means close

Tabs are not history. Back closes the top sheet, then leaves a pushed screen (schedule detail slides in from the right and slides back out). It never jumps between tabs.

Search is a layer

Tapping search covers the screen with the keyboard up and no animation. Cancel returns you to exactly where you were.

Thumb reach

Add, Auto or Build, and the sheet handles sit in the bottom third. Remove and delete stay one level down, in the sheet or the ⋯ menu.

One fact per row

A row is a name and the one fact you act on in that tab. Sections, instructors and rooms wait in the sheet.
